# Genre calibration

The six audits do not apply with equal force everywhere. A course lesson must state
its point; an essay does not. Calibrate before rewriting. The short-text rule: under
~400 words, focus on audits 1 (theme), 3 (emotion mode), 4 (specificity), and 6
(shape convergence); structural moves like tangents and time jumps need room.

## Course lessons (Skool / solo-scale-writer)

Teaching demands explicitness. The tell is not that the lesson is stated; it is that
it gets restated as a moral at the end of every section.

- State the concept once, clearly, early. Do not close each section by re-deriving it.
- "Bottom line" sections in at most 1 of every 3 lessons (palette rotation already
  enforces skeleton variety; keep it).
- Name every real tool, price, and version (already rule 6 in solo-scale-writer).
- Plain emotion language in anecdotes; no body-performance.
- Allowed interventions: the named thing, plain emotion, open thread ("we'll hit this
  wall again in module 3 and fix it there"), occasional cold open.
- Skip: heavy nonlinearity (pedagogy needs sequence), oblique tangents (attention
  budget is tight).

## LinkedIn posts (writing-linkedin-posts)

Short, native genre norms include direct address and a takeaway, so the tell is
uniformity: every post shaped scene -> tension -> turning point -> stated insight.

- Insight stated at most once. Roughly a third of posts should not state it at all;
  end on the moment or the number and let comments do the interpreting.
- Rotate skeletons: outcome-first, mid-scene open, delayed-number reveal,
  unstated-lesson. Never the same shape twice in a row.
- Named specifics are the cheapest human marker here: real client names (when
  allowed), real dollar amounts, real tools, real dates.
- Plain emotion: "honestly, that one stung" beats "my stomach dropped".
- Skip: fourth-wall moves beyond one light touch (reads as gimmick at this length).

## Blog posts / essays (jens-blog-writer, ghostwriting)

Full menu available. This is where structural humanity pays most.

- One deliberate structural choice per post: nonlinear open, recontextualization
  callback, oblique tangent, or open thread. Pick per piece, vary across pieces.
- No epilogue coda. When the piece lands, stop. (Claude fingerprint: cut the
  wrap-up paragraph after the natural ending.)
- Ambivalent closes allowed and encouraged where genuine: both feelings intact,
  no resolution forced.
- Vary event intensity across the piece; flat evenness is a Claude tell.
- The systems-angle section (Jens structure) must not become a ritual slot; some
  posts skip it, some open with it.

## Emails / newsletters (Modernpreneur, beehiiv)

- Lesson once, near the top or bottom, not both.
- The PS is a natural oblique-tangent slot; use it as one, not as a second CTA.
- Named specifics over category words ("Instantly at $37/mo" not "a cold email tool").
- Body-performance emotion reads especially fake at email intimacy; name feelings.
- Skip: nonlinear structure (email scanning behavior punishes it).

## Everything, always

- Audit 6 (shape convergence) applies to every genre: check the skeleton against the
  last 2-3 pieces in the same channel before publishing.
- Never deploy the full intervention menu in one piece. 1-2 moves, chosen on purpose.
